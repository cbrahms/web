Calibration

What this sweep did not find

Because this dashboard exists to test a specific and unusual claim — that wealthy actors know something the public does not — it should be explicit about the negative result.

No signal in this sweep constitutes evidence of foreknowledge of a specific event. Every actor cited has published a generic, sufficient and self-consistent rationale for their behavior: valuations are high, the dollar is a concentrated risk, the Middle East is at war, and reserve assets have been demonstrated to be seizable. Those explanations are adequate on their own.

What is genuinely anomalous is the synchronization and magnitude of de-risking against a backdrop of record complacency. That is a real, measurable and worth-watching condition. It is not a secret.
