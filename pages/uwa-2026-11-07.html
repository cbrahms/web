Inference. The positioning signals in issues 006–008 (record Goldman tech selling, Morgan Stanley de-grossing, private-credit queues) preceded the index move by two to three weeks. A sharp drop without a macro trigger fits forced deleveraging by crowded holders. The early movers were the better-informed. The index move is the transmission, not a new signal.
