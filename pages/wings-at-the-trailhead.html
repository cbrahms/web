Wings at the Trailhead — CR-105 Storyboard

Cryptid Rescue Shorts — CR-105 — Storyboard

"Every November since: wings at the trailhead."

Cryptid Mothman · Duration 58s · Style Flanagan register · Arc Love · Shots 8

01 — DREAD (7s)

CAM: Slow push-in past the bench toward the ridge, low angle, shallow depth of field shifting focus from plaque to distant red lights

November trailhead at blue-hour dusk, bare trees, cold mist. A weathered memorial bench, brass plaque catching the last light. Above the dark ridge, two ember-red points hover where no towers stand.

VO: "First November without her, I hiked our trail anyway. Grief does that — walks the old routes, looking for the person in the scenery." (low, tired, unafraid)

02 — PURSUIT (7s)

CAM: Handheld tracking alongside the man, trees strobing past in foreground, red eyes held in soft focus background

A hollow-eyed man climbs a switchback in near-dark forest, headlamp off, breath visible. Between the black trunks, two ember-red eyes pace him, always level, always watching.

VO: "People ask if I was terrified. You have to have something left to be terrified with. That year, the tank was empty." (flat, honest)

03 — GLIMPSE — SILENT BEAT (8s)

CAM: Static wide, locked-off. Man small at frame bottom, creature towering at frame top, long held composition

The overlook. It is there — seven feet, matte charcoal-gray, wings folded wrong, one pinned under collapsed chain-link from the old fire tower. Two steady red eyes. It does not move.

VO: — silence —

04 — THE TURN (6s)

CAM: Macro close-up, slow rack focus from torn wing membrane up to the waiting red eyes

Extreme close-up of the pinned wing: torn velvety membrane, rust-stained fence wire biting into soot-dark fur. In the blurred upper frame, red eyes wait with resigned patience.

VO: "It had her look. March, the last hospital month — the look of someone done asking. I couldn't fix that look then. God help me, I could fix it now." (cracking softly)

05 — APPROACH (8s)

CAM: Over-the-shoulder medium from behind the wing, slow subtle drift, red eye-light as key light

He kneels bare-handed, bending rusted wire from the pinned wing, lips moving in quiet speech. The creature's eyes stay fixed on his face, inches away, calm.

VO: "Annie used to say: hands busy, voice low, and don't you dare cry yet. So that's what I did. That's what I had." (tender, ritual)

06 — THE ACT (8s)

CAM: Tight two-shot in profile, static, shallow focus, faint breath and heat distortion between the two faces

Freed and full height, it leans down until its forehead almost touches his. Heat-shimmer off its body like a banked fire. His eyes close, face cracking with grief and relief.

VO: "It leaned. Like it knew the shape of what was hollow in me, and stood in the doorway of it. Just for a second. Just long enough." (barely voiced)

07 — TRANSFORMATION (7s)

CAM: Low-angle wide from behind the man, tilt up as the wings unfurl, then hold on the empty dark after it vanishes

It steps off the edge, opens enormous ragged wings that blot the stars. Eyes pulse once, soft and deliberate, then it drops silently into the black valley.

VO: "I cried on the way down. Good crying. The kind that's finally about the person, not the absence." (exhale, first small smile)

08 — THE COST (7s)

CAM: Slow pull-back and gentle tilt up from the feather on the plaque to the red lights over the ridge, ending wide

One year later, same bench, two thermoses out of habit. Laid across the brass plaque: one enormous wing-feather, placed like a hand. Above the ridge, two red lights wait in the deepening blue.

VO: "Every November since, wings at the trailhead. We hike her trail together, it and I. Neither of us says her name. Both of us mean it." (warm, complete)
