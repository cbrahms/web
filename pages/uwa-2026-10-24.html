Assessment

The scan shows convergence of heavy insider selling, multi-decade-high Treasury yields and gating in wealth-channel private credit. Each has a conventional explanation: planned sales after large gains, a hawkish Fed with heavy issuance, and a post-fraud credit repricing. Together they describe elevated financial strain and cautious positioning by insiders, not a coherent multi-actor exit.

The scan does not show rumours of foreknowledge, unusual asset purchases such as bunkers, coordinated hedge-fund moves (Q3 13Fs are unpublished) or political-trading anomalies. Changes from the prior scan are marginal. Mainstream coverage of each item exists; the gap is in connecting them, not in suppression.

SIG-02 | Hard data | Treasury yields at multi-decade highs: 10-year about 5.3% at the 7 Oct auction, 30-year about 5.62% on 8 Oct

What: 10-year reopening cleared near 5.30% (highest since 2002 per aggregator; 5.23% vs 5.30% figures conflict). 30-year priced about 5.618% with bid-to-cover 2.54 (above 2.41 average), indirect bidders over 70%. SIG-03 | Corroborated | Private-credit redemption pressure: first net outflow at perpetual BDCs, gating at major managers, non-accruals rising to 2.8%

What: Fed May 2026 report: perpetual BDC redemptions exceeded inflows in Q1. Q1 requests above $13B, funds met about two-thirds. Blackstone BCRED raised its tender cap 5% to 7% on about $3.7B requests; BlackRock capped HLEND; Blue Owl halted redemptions at OBDC II. Non-accruals at the 20 largest listed BDCs rose to a median 2.8% in Q2 from 2.0% in March.

Why unusual: Illiquid wealth-channel vehicles gating while non-accruals rise is the conventional early shape of a credit-cycle turn; wealthy retail and family offices are the marginal holders.

Conventional read: Fed called it manageable; the largest ten BDCs can absorb about three quarters of capped redemptions; no bank failures found. Several sources are secondary and none is October-dated.

Sources: Long Angle: Private Credit Perspectives Q1 2026 · Family Wealth Report: private credit's recent stresses · stock3: private credit stress test

Conventional read: Survey intent often exceeds action; gold fell from its August peak under a hawkish Fed.
