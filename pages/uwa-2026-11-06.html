Assessment

Fact. Hedge funds, corporate insiders, private-wealth credit investors and central banks each moved defensively in the six weeks to 6 Nov. Each move is documented by a different data source.

Inference. The pattern is convergent in timing and direction. Its common driver is rates: the 30-year peaked at a 24-year high on 7 Oct, and allocators name disorderly yields as the top tail risk. The broad market and credit spreads have not followed. Leading holders are de-risking ahead of the index.

Not claimed. Coordination among actors. Foreknowledge of any specific event. A forecast of a crash date.

Reversal conditions. Any two lowers the verdict to emerging. Status at this run:

1. 12 Nov 30-year auction clears below 5.3% with indirect bid above 68%. Trending toward met: secondary 5.28% on 4 Nov.

2. Nvidia beats and raises on 17 Nov and hedge-fund gross exposure recovers. Pending.

3. December hike odds fall below 50%. Not met: about 85% after the 28 Oct decision. No later reading retrieved.
