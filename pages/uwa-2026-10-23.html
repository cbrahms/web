Assessment

The scan finds persistence, not escalation. Insider selling, cash accumulation (Berkshire) and a bond-focused tail-risk consensus point the same direction as in August, and the rate backdrop (10-year 5.27%, $1.99T deficit) is worse. Each element has a conventional explanation: pre-planned sales after a long rally, high cash yields, and an openly debated fiscal path.

What it is not: evidence of coordination, of any actor holding specific foreknowledge, or of a new instrument class being used. Social and forum channels were not accessible and no rumor language surfaced. The one structural concern is the interaction of a leveraged Treasury basis trade with a yield spike, which is a public-data fragility, not a secret.

Verdict is emerging because two or more signals converge (insider net selling plus rate-risk positioning) without a coherent multi-actor pattern beyond what was already flagged.
