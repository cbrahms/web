Assessment

The scan again shows three converging, publicly observable signals: elevated insider selling, long-dated Treasury yields at 25-year highs, and accelerated official gold purchases. Each has a strong conventional explanation (10b5-1 plans, term premium, reserve diversification), and no new actor, instrument or timing link emerged this run.

The pattern is not a coordinated move, and nothing here evidences knowledge of a specific event. It is a defensive-leaning backdrop that persists. Evidence this run was mostly carried over; the October auction, Q3 13Fs and hedge-fund positioning could not be retrieved.

Reversal conditions: a benign 28 Oct Fed outcome with easing long yields, or Q3 13Fs showing hedge funds and Berkshire adding equity.
